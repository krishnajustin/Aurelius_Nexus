Common Questions

Frequently
asked.

What's actually included in onboarding? +

A 60-minute strategy call, a written project brief, a shared workspace (Notion or Linear), access to your dedicated team channel, and a delivery roadmap with milestones — all within the first week.

How long does a typical project take? +

Starter projects ship in 2–3 weeks. Professional builds run 6–10 weeks. Enterprise engagements span quarters and are planned in phased releases.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes. Most engagements split into 40% on signing, 40% at midpoint review, and 20% on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.

What happens after the project ships? +

Every tier includes a post-launch care window. After that, we offer optional monthly retainers covering monitoring, performance, content updates, and continuous improvement.

Can I mix services across tiers? +

Absolutely. The tiers are starting points, not boxes. Most engagements are scoped à la carte — start the journey and we'll tailor it together.
